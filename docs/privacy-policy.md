# Donuts for Steven: privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. This policy covers the Android game "Donuts for Steven"
(package `com.donuts.game`).

## The short version

Donuts for Steven collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no chat,
no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send anything
anywhere.

## What the game keeps on the phone

To pick up where your child left off, the game saves a few things in its own private storage on the phone:

- the board as it was left, and the donut counter for that game;
- the total donuts popped, the best game on each board size, the longest chain, the number of times the
  game was opened, and which stickers and donut friends have been earned;
- the settings: sound, buzz, hint timing and board size, and whether the how-to-play hint was seen.

None of it is personal information. There is no name, photo, voice, location, contact, device identifier or
advertising ID. Other apps cannot read it, and the developer never sees it.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in the phone's settings, deletes all of it. Inside the game,
**Reset stickers** in the settings clears the totals and the stickers (the donut friends stay).

## Phone backups

If the phone's owner has Android backup turned on, Android may copy the saved progress above into that
owner's own Google account backup, so it comes back on a new phone. That copy is made and held by Android
under the family's account. It is never sent to the developer, and turning backup off in the phone's
settings stops it.

## Permissions

The game asks for one permission, **vibrate**, so the phone can buzz when donuts pop. The buzz can be turned
off in the game's settings.

## Children

The game is made for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not
collect any from children, and there is nothing to share, sell or disclose. It contains no links out of the
app, no social features and nothing to buy.

## Changes

If a future version ever needed to collect or send anything, this policy would be updated and the change
explained in the release notes before that version was published. The date at the top shows the last change.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
